title: The new "command center" look, and cloud backup that brings your pharmacy back on a new phone
date: 2026-10-07
version: "0.7.0"
status: completed
---

- A completely new dark "command center" design, with a light mode one tap away on the sign-in screen or in the admin settings.
- A soft green flash around the screen edge with a gentle vibration whenever an operation completes: a sale, a return, saving a product, receiving stock and more.
- A daily encrypted backup of the admin device on the PharmFlow server, which only your license code can open.
- Lost your phone? Install the app on a new one, choose "Have a license code? Activate or restore your pharmacy", paste your code, and your accounts and data come back as they were — the old phone is stopped automatically.
- A reminder on the admin dashboard when daily backup is off, turned on with one tap.
- A safe recovery screen if the device's data cannot be opened, instead of silently starting an empty database.
