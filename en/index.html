Product introduction

What is PharmFlow?

A program for running the pharmacy day to day: fast sales, batch-level stock and automatic blocking of expired medicine. It keeps its data encrypted on your device and can optionally sync it between the devices of a branch.

Pharmacy owners

A scheduled daily report by email or Telegram, a full invoice archive, management of the team, devices and permissions, and a license tied to the branch.

Pharmacists

Stock by batch and expiry date, alerts for near-expiry and low quantity, bulk entry of new stock from a file or an invoice photo, and returns handling.

Pharmacy staff

One sales screen: scan the barcode, review the cart, choose the payment method, complete the sale and print the receipt in as few steps as possible.

System overview

What PharmFlow manages

PharmFlow is an offline-first pharmacy program for Android and Windows (version 0.6.0+7). It keeps its data in an encrypted database on the device and organizes sales, stock, expiry and reports.

Products

Name, unit, barcode and retail price, entered one by one or in bulk.

Batches

Each batch has a number, an expiry date and a quantity, so stock is tracked per batch.

Invoices

Every sale is saved as a numbered invoice with its items and payment method.

Branch and team

Devices of one branch can be linked with optional sync, with team and device management.

Secure by design

The database is encrypted with SQLCipher, the PIN is stored using an Argon2id hash, and the license is signed with Ed25519.

Point of sale

How a sale happens

From one screen: scan the barcode with the camera, type it, or search by name, review the cart, then complete the sale. If the barcode is unknown an "Add this product" button appears.

Scanning and search

  • The device camera is the scanner, with no separate device needed.
  • Reads QR, DataMatrix, EAN-13, EAN-8, Code 128, Code 39 and UPC-A.
  • Typing the barcode and searching by name are available as alternatives.

Sales cart

  • Several items, with quantities you raise or lower with a button.
  • An immediate warning if the selected batch is close to expiry.
  • A manual invoice discount, and line-price editing for pharmacists and admins only.
  • Cash, card or credit payment, then a PDF receipt.

What happens automatically

  • The batch nearest to expiry (FEFO) is chosen each time you add an item.
  • Expired batches cannot be sold.
  • The quantity is deducted from that same batch and a stock movement is recorded.

Hold the cart

Hold the cart to serve another customer and resume it later from the held-carts list; it stays saved on the device.

Expiry management (FEFO)

Steps of a sale

From scan to receipt

The real sales flow inside the app, step by step.

  1. 1

    Scan or search

    Add the item by scanning the barcode, typing it, or searching by name.

    • The camera reads the barcode instantly, with no button press
    • For an unknown product an "Add this product" button appears
    • Adding a new product is available to pharmacists and admins
  2. 2

    Batch selection (FEFO)

    The system automatically picks the batch nearest to expiry.

    • No manual entry
    • A colored warning if expiry is close
    • Expired batches cannot be sold
  3. 3

    Review the cart

    Change the quantity or remove a line, and add a manual discount if needed.

    • Raise or lower the quantity with a button
    • Removing a line asks for confirmation so it is not erased by mistake
    • Price editing for pharmacists and admins only
  4. 4

    Pay and complete

    Choose cash, card or credit, then complete the sale.

    • A numbered invoice with its items is saved
    • The quantity is deducted from the selected batch
    • Print the receipt or share it as a PDF

Note

A return is a separate operation that does not edit the original invoice and puts the quantity back into its original batch.

Smart entry features

  • Gudea label reading

    Scan the QR code printed on an Iraqi drug pack and its data is filled in automatically: trade and generic name, dosage form, batch number and expiry date, with a warning when a drug is blocked.

    Needs an internet connection; optional, it never blocks manual entry.

  • AI pack photo

    Photograph the pack and AI extracts the product name and manufacturer, plus the generic name and strength when printed. It leaves a field empty rather than guessing, and marks filled fields for review.

    Needs a connection and a Gemini key.

  • Smart bulk entry

    Attach a supplier invoice as an image or PDF and it becomes rows you review before committing, or import an Excel/CSV file directly with no AI and automatic column mapping. Each row is classed as a new product or a restock.

  • Smart barcode generation

    For a product without a barcode the app generates a valid local code in one tap, produces print-ready labels as a PDF, and fills them from the last bulk entry automatically. It never creates a label for a product that already has a real barcode.

Feature

Offline mode

Everything a cashier and pharmacist need runs on the device itself, so work does not stop when the internet drops.

  • Sales, stock, invoices and expiry alerts work with no connection at all.
  • Optional cloud sync when the internet is available, to link the devices of one branch.
  • Whatever is waiting to be sent is queued and re-sent automatically when the connection returns.

Inventory management

Stock tracked per batch: each batch has its own number, expiry date and quantity, and every product shows a colored status.

  • Four tabs: all, low, near expiry and expired, with instant search.
  • Bulk stock entry from an Excel/CSV file or a supplier invoice photo, reviewed before it is committed.
  • A price-change log and a reconciliation report that compares stock across branch devices.

Barcode & QR

Barcode & QR reading

The camera is the scanner, so no separate scanning device is needed.

  • Reads QR, DataMatrix, EAN-13, EAN-8, Code 128, Code 39 and UPC-A.
  • Reads Iraqi Gudea labels to fill in drug details automatically (optional, needs a connection).
  • Generates a local code for a product with no barcode and prints its labels on an L7160 sheet as a PDF.
Currently available

Invoices

Invoices & returns

Every sale is saved as a numbered invoice that does not change, and a return is a separate operation linked to it.

  • Numbered invoices with their items and payment method, with an archive for the admin.
  • Full or partial returns put the quantity back into its original batch without editing the original invoice.
  • A scheduled daily report delivered by email or Telegram.
Currently available

Security

Security & permissions

Pharmacy data is encrypted on the device, and permissions are split by role.

  • The database is encrypted with SQLCipher, and the PIN is stored using an Argon2id hash.
  • Three roles: cashier, pharmacist and admin, and each role sees only the buttons it is allowed to use.
  • A digitally signed license (Ed25519) tied to the branch, and management of the registered devices.
Currently available
